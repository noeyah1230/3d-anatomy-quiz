# Keyboard controls

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to navigate controls. Enter and Space activate focused buttons. Camera presets, reset and zoom all have buttons. Drag and pinch use pointer input.

## Typing practice

- **Enter** checks a nonblank answer. After feedback, focus moves to **Next question** or **See results** and Enter activates it.
- Space and arrows work normally in the answer field. Enter during IME composition leaves the answer unsubmitted.
- Outside text inputs and other controls, **H** toggles the hint, **R** resets the view, and **A** starts or pauses orbit. After feedback, **Right Arrow** also advances.

## Flashcards

- **Space** reveals or hides the answer when focus is outside another control.
- **1**, **2**, **3**, and **4** record **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, and **Easy** after the answer is visible. The rating advances to the next card.
- **H**, **R**, and **A** use the same behavior as in typing practice. Orbit starts paused and runs only when requested.

In the ungraded preview, use **Reveal model label** or **Reveal answer**, then **Next question**. The last item uses **Finish preview**.
